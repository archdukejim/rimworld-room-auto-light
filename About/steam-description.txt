[h1]Room Auto Light[/h1]

Your colonists do not walk around flicking every lamp in a room one at a time, and neither should you. Room Auto Light treats each room's lamps as [b]one aggregated switch[/b]: they come up together, they go dark together, and while they are dark the room draws nothing from your grid.

[h2]What it does[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Walk in and the room lights up.[/b] The whole group comes on within a quarter second of someone entering, on the same tick — not one lamp at a time over several seconds the way the vanilla power net restores things.
[*][b]Leave and it goes dark[/b], after a short delay so a pawn passing through does not strobe the place.
[*][b]Sleepers count as nobody.[/b] A bedroom goes dark once its occupants are asleep and lights back up the moment one of them wakes.
[*][b]Power is aggregated, all or nothing.[/b] A room only lights up if the grid can pay for every one of its lamps at once, so it never comes up half lit. Select any lamp and the room reports as a single load: lamp count, wattage, current state, and why.
[*][b]Move a wall and the circuit trips.[/b] A blast, a deconstruction or a new wall changes the room under its lamps: they drop at once and stay down until you reset them.
[*][b]Any lamp can be pulled out.[/b] Ungroup a lamp and it goes back to plain vanilla behaviour, keeping its own power and its own switch.
[*][b]Quiet.[/b] No power click every time a room lights itself. Flicking a lamp by hand still clicks, exactly as vanilla does.
[/list]

[h2]Outdoor lamps[/h2]

Every lamp you own standing outdoors forms [b]one single group[/b], rather than the mod trying to automate the map-sized outdoor "room". It runs [b]on darkness[/b] by default, reading how dark it actually is rather than the clock, so an eclipse or a black storm at noon brings your perimeter up exactly like nightfall does.

[h2]Grow rooms[/h2]

Sun lamps are left alone: vanilla already powers them down for the plant resting period. What this mod covers is the gap that leaves behind. A grow room's ordinary lamps [b]stand down while the sun lamp is lit[/b] — nothing a 30W lamp adds is visible next to a sun lamp — and take back over the moment it rests, so whoever is still harvesting at 8pm is not doing it in the dark.

[h2]The controls[/h2]

Select any lamp and you get the switches for its whole group. Select several lamps from the same room and they collapse into one set.

[h3]Room lights[/h3]
Switches every lamp in the room as one. While off, the group draws nothing from the grid.
[list]
[*][b]auto[/b] — occupancy drives the group.
[*][b]always on[/b] — the group is held lit.
[*][b]always off[/b] — the group is held dark.
[/list]

[h3]Schedule[/h3]
While a schedule is set it is the [i]only[/i] thing deciding, so occupants are ignored. Always on and always off still override it.
[list]
[*][b]off[/b] — no schedule. Occupancy drives the group.
[*][b]dusk to dawn[/b] — on at dusk, off at dawn. Runs on the celestial clock, so weather and eclipses are ignored and a dark storm at noon will not light it.
[*][b]on darkness[/b] — runs on how dark it actually is, so an eclipse or a black storm at noon lights the group just like nightfall does.
[/list]

[h3]Sleepers[/h3]
Never applied outdoors.
[list]
[*][b]dark if any asleep[/b] — one sleeper is enough to send the room dark even with others awake, which suits a bedroom shared with a workshop corner.
[*][b]dark if all asleep[/b] — the room goes dark once every occupant is asleep, and lights back up the moment one of them wakes.
[*][b]ignored[/b] — sleepers count as ordinary occupants and hold the lights on, which suits a hospital or a barracks you want lit around the clock.
[/list]

[h3]Reset lighting circuit[/h3]
Appears only on a lamp whose circuit is down after a room change. One click repairs every broken lamp in that room and re-forms the group around the room as it now stands. A [b]Lighting circuit damaged[/b] alert lists the affected lamps, so clicking it cycles through them and nothing sits forgotten in the dark.

[h3]Ungroup lamp[/h3]
Per lamp rather than per group. Pulls one lamp out of its room group and hands it back to vanilla, so it keeps its own power and its own switch. Use it for a lamp you always want lit, or to keep one lamp out of the all-or-nothing power check when the room genuinely cannot afford every lamp at once. The switch stays on the lamp while it is out, so it can rejoin.

Every choice is per room and survives saving, reloading, and rebuilt walls.

[h2]Settings[/h2]

Animals as occupants, default sleeper handling, all-or-nothing power and its retry delay, whether room changes break circuits, the delay before a room goes dark, the re-evaluation interval, the glow levels that count as dusk and as dark, outdoor grouping, a wattage ceiling, and defName lists to force lamps in or out.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]RimWorld 1.6.[/b]
[*][b]Safe to add mid-save.[/b] Removing it hands every lamp straight back to vanilla.
[*][b]Modded lamps work out of the box.[/b] Anything that is a plain building with a glower and a power trader is picked up automatically. Lamps with a custom class can be added by defName in settings.
[*][b]Plays nicely with the power net.[/b] Lamps are held off through the same check vanilla itself uses before re-powering anything, so there is no per-tick tug of war, no fake flicking, and no "needs power" icons cluttering your base.
[*][b]Light on the tick.[/b] One room is evaluated at a time, rooms are spread across the window, and nothing in the steady path allocates.
[/list]

[h2]Performance[/h2]

Benchmarked in game rather than guessed at, on a purpose-built colony of [b]100 rooms and 400 lamps[/b] with colonists driven through it on a fixed schedule. It costs about [b]0.25% of a 60 TPS tick budget[/b], and the cost scales with how often rooms change state rather than with how many lamps you own — a large colony whose lamps rarely change is close to free.

Full method, results, and the reasoning behind both:
https://github.com/archdukejim/rimworld-room-auto-light/wiki/Performance

[h2]Change log[/h2]

[b]1.0.1[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Silent switching.[/b] RimWorld plays the power click — the same sound as a power failure — whenever anything gains or loses power. Fine for a switch you throw by hand; not fine several times a minute, forever, for every room in the colony. Every switch this mod makes is now silent, on every path. Hand flicks still click. Can be turned off in settings.
[*][b]Room switches cost half what they did.[/b] 1627.8 us down to 796.4 us per switch. The engine invalidates glow per lamp over the lamp's whole lit area, and four lamps in one room have nearly the same area — so a room switch was marking about 2500 cells to cover a union of about 730. A switch now collects the cells and hands the distinct set over once.
[*][b]Mean tick cost down 35%[/b], 59.1 us to 38.4 us, and the worst tick down 2.5x, 4354 us to 1746 us.
[*][b]Rebuilds cost about a third of what they did[/b], and are debounced to at most one per 30 ticks — walls coming down during a raid previously forced a full rebuild every single tick.
[*][b]No allocation when a building is selected.[/b] The gizmo hook was allocating for every selected building each frame, lamp or not.
[/list]

[b]1.0[/b]
[list]
[*]Rooms are driven as one group: every lamp switches on the same tick, rather than trickling on the way the vanilla power net restores things.
[*]All-or-nothing power: a room only lights if the grid can pay for every lamp at once.
[*]Schedules: dusk to dawn on the celestial clock, or on darkness using real sky glow.
[*]Outdoor lamps form a single group, running on darkness by default.
[*]Sleepers darken a room, on a rule you set per room.
[*]Grow rooms hand over between the sun lamp and the ordinary lamps.
[*]Room changes trip the circuit, with an alert and a per-room reset.
[*]Ungroup any lamp to hand it back to vanilla.
[/list]
